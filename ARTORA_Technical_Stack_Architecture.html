1. Executive Summary & Architecture Overview

ARTORA is a high-end, editorial 3D online art marketplace designed for contemporary artists, international art collectors, and emerging creators. Unlike conventional templated e-commerce storefronts, ARTORA fuses high-performance WebGL 3D immersion with a refined editorial aesthetic and a robust full-stack marketplace architecture.

3. 3D Graphics & WebGL Implementation Detail

The 3D design in ARTORA is engineered as a core interactive medium rather than a static background video:

A. Interactive Floating Hero (HeroScene3D.tsx)

• Custom 4-Point Studio Lighting: Soft ambient light (0xfff8ee), overhead key spotlight (SpotLight), cold cobalt rim light (0x435BFF), and warm burnt-orange point fill (0xD96A45).
• PBR Physics: Physically Based MeshStandardMaterial with roughness and metalness mapping.
• Fluid Cursor Parallax: Smooth mouse tracking with inertia damping (targetX += (mouseX - targetX) * 0.055).
• Trigonometric Float: Harmonic floating oscillations across 5 distinct framed masterworks at varying z-depths.

B. Virtual Exhibition Pavilion (VirtualGalleryRoom.tsx)

• Full 360° Room Mesh: 4-wall architectural gallery room with natural floor reflections and exhibition lighting fixtures.
• Raycasting & Hover Hit-Testing: Three.js Raycaster detecting pointer collision on artwork frames.
• Artwork Inspection Drawer: Clicking frames opens high-resolution provenance, dimensions, and quick-purchase drawer.
• Guided Tour Camera Tweening: Automated camera transitions between exhibition walls with acoustic toggle.

4. Backend, State Engine & E-Commerce Logic

ARTORA is architected with a production-ready client backend (AppContext.tsx) that guarantees persistence, role permissions, and e-commerce transactions:

Subsystem Implementation Strategy Business & Platform Value
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Instant dual-persona switcher between buyer (Tara Mehta) and artist (Elena Rostova). Enables testing both collector checkout flows and artist studio management without creating multiple accounts.
1-of-1 Original Inventory Lock Strict inventory guard checking artworkType === 'original' against active cart & orders. Guarantees exclusive original masterworks cannot be double-sold. Automatically locks inventory on checkout.
Multi-Currency Conversion Real-time exchange multiplier (1 INR = 0.012 USD = 0.011 EUR) with dynamic locale formatting. Global collectors can browse and purchase in their local currency seamlessly with synchronized state.
E-Commerce Escrow Flow 3-tier checkout (Shipping Address, Payment Method via Razorpay/Stripe/UPI, Confirmation). Simulates escrow payment release, buyer protection guarantee, and order confirmation generation.
Order Fulfillment Pipeline Sequential order generator (ART-2025-XXXX) with status pipeline (confirmed → in_preparation → shipped). Generates trackable shipping numbers (BlueDart / DHL) and digital Certificate of Authenticity.
State Persistence & Hydration JSON serialization into browser localStorage with safety fallbacks and initial seed data. Cart items, wishlist, user preferences, created artwork listings, and order history survive page reloads.
